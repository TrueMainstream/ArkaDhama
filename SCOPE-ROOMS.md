# Arka Dhama — room reservation system

**Scope and fee.** Prepared 11 November 2026, Mysore.

This document covers one system: guests book a room on the Arka Dhama website and pay there. It is the first project. The restaurant ordering system is a separate project, quoted separately, and is not included here.

The property for this build is **10 rooms in 3 types**.

---

## What is included

### The guest page

- Works on a phone, a tablet, and a computer.
- Shows the room types, each with photographs, what is included, the house rules, and the price per night.
- The guest picks dates and sees which types are free.
- The guest enters a name and a contact, pays a deposit or the full stay, and gets a confirmation by email and on WhatsApp.
- No phone call and no message to the desk. The page is the booking.
- Two guests cannot buy the last room. One gets it. The other is told on the page that it has just gone, and is refunded if their payment already went through.

### The desk page

Staff sign in. Guests do not.

- Arrivals, departures, and who has paid.
- Block dates, for a guru's guest, maintenance, or a program week.
- Type in a booking for someone standing at the desk.
- Change the nightly price of each room type.
- Set a second price for a date range, such as a program week. A price change applies to new bookings. A booking already paid keeps the price it was sold at.

Changing prices is part of this fee. It is not an extra.

The three types and the ten rooms are set up as part of the build.

If construction later adds rooms of those same three types, I update the count at no charge. A room of a type that already exists is a number, not a new system, whether it is one room or a new wing.

### Payment

- UPI, including PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm.
- Indian debit and credit cards, including RuPay.
- International cards, for guests who flew in.
- Razorpay takes the payment. Card numbers stay with Razorpay. They are never stored on Arka Dhama's system.
- Razorpay's own percentage, and GST on that percentage, is their cost. It is not part of the fee below.

PayPal is not included. It does not take a domestic Indian payment.

### The existing website

- WordPress on SiteGround stays up. It is not rebuilt, and the hosting is not cancelled.
- The domain registration stays where it is.
- The nameservers move to Cloudflare, so the booking page can live on the same domain. This is reversible. It is not a transfer of the domain.

### After launch

- Hosting of the booking system is about USD 5 a month, paid by Arka Dhama to Cloudflare.
- For 30 days after the first live paid booking, defects are fixed at no charge. A defect is a room sold twice, a confirmation that did not send, or a payment taken with no room. A new feature is not a defect.

## How payment works

Guests in India pay by UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) or by Indian card, including RuPay. Guests who have flown in pay by international card.

Payment is handled by Razorpay. Card numbers are stored by Razorpay, never on Arka Dhama's system. The fee below does not include Razorpay's own charge, which is a percentage of each successful payment and is confirmed by Arka Dhama's accountant.

PayPal is not part of this project. It does not accept a domestic Indian payment.

## What happens to the existing website

The current WordPress site on SiteGround stays. It is not rebuilt and the hosting is not cancelled.

The domain name stays with whoever holds it today. What changes is where the domain is looked up: the nameservers move to Cloudflare, so the existing site keeps working and the new booking page can live on the same domain. This is a short, reversible step. It is not a transfer of ownership of the domain.

## Adding room types from the desk

The fee above sets up the three types. Staff can change their prices. Staff cannot invent a fourth type, rewrite its description, or replace its photographs without me.

If Arka Dhama wants that freedom, the desk page also lets staff:

- Add, edit, or hide a room type: name, description, photographs, what is included, how many guests.
- Add rooms under a type, or take one room out of service, without asking me.
- Keep the same price controls: a nightly price, and a different price on a date range.

**USD 1,500** added to this project if agreed before building starts. The total is then **USD 7,500**, on the same split: half on agreement, half when a real booking has been paid and confirmed.

Without that option, a new kind of room (a fourth type, not more rooms of the existing three) is added by me after launch for **USD 250** per type. They supply the photographs and the description. This is the construction case where the new building is not the same as the rooms already on the page.

Buying the option means they never pay the USD 250. They add the new type themselves, including after future construction.

This does not become a second property, a channel manager, or a new language. Those stay out.

## What is not included

These are real requests, and they are later projects. They are not quietly added to this one.

- The restaurant ordering system. Menu, cart, kitchen screen, pickup and delivery.
- Listing the rooms on Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Agoda, or Airbnb.
- A mobile app in the App Store or Play Store. The page is built for a phone browser.
- Door locks, ID scanning, housekeeping, a night audit, or accounting software.
- Promo codes, discounts per guest, or automatic price changes based on demand.
- Rewriting the ashram website, the teachings, or a donation page.
- Kannada or Hindi. English is this version. Another language is a later addition.
- A second property.

### What the same thing costs elsewhere

A rented small-hotel system (Little Hotelier is the usual one at this size) runs roughly USD 39 to USD 150 a month, plus a booking fee, and it is never Arka Dhama's. Published US prices for having a booking website built run from about USD 10,000 for a simple reservation page to USD 60,000 and above for a custom booking engine (figures from public agency guides, 2026). This property does not need the engine. It needs one property, three room types, and payment that works in India. USD 6,000 is below those US builds, and it is a system Arka Dhama owns.
