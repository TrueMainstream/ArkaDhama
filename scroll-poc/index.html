01

Body

The ground under the teaching. Breath, posture, the place itself.

Threshold

Arrive before you begin.

Shoes off. The corridor is cooler than the courtyard. Nothing here asks to be understood yet.

Practice

Sit until the room sits with you.

Arka Dhyana starts in the body. Keep scrolling. This channel widens on its own, and the other two give way.

The opening

The courtyard, bank to bank.

No second layer. The column itself is the wide scene, once the scroll has carried it there.

End of Body. Scroll back and the channel narrows.
02

Mind

Attention, study, the long argument with yourself.

Study

A book is a slow door.

Read one page. Close it. The point was never to finish.

Inquiry

Notice which thought you obey.

The banks bow before they straighten. The middle of the channel opens ahead of the edges.

The opening

One argument, the full width.

Only this scroll does it. Body and Spirit cannot widen this channel by catching up.

End of Mind.
03

Spirit

What remains when the practice is no longer an effort.

Stillness

Not above the other two.

Spirit is the third channel, not the prize. It does not outrank Body or Mind.

Offering

A lamp is enough light.

Still no video. The colour is a stand-in for a photograph cropped to the channel, then to the full stage.

The opening

The hall, shore to shore.

Scroll on. The other two thin out, and this one settles into a single quiet width.

End of Spirit.
